This policy applies to free mobile games published by Pat Guettler / Grapegames on Google Play and related storefronts, including Unicorn Arcade, Peregrine, Pigeon, and any future apps under the same developer account unless a separate policy is linked for that app.

Who We Are

These apps are built and published by Pat Guettler (Grapegames). Contact: grapegames3141@gmail.com.

Information Stored on Your Device

The apps save game progress and preferences locally on your device. Depending on the game, that may include high scores, level progress, sound settings, in-game currency or unlocks earned by playing, and similar play state.

This information is not sent to Grapegames servers. I do not operate user accounts for these games, and I do not run my own analytics or crash-reporting backends for them.

Third-Party Services

The apps may use Google services, including:

  • Google Play (distribution, updates, and related platform services)
  • Google AdMob (banner or other ads, when enabled in that app)

Those services may process device and app-interaction data under their own policies. See:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob & privacy
  • Google ad controls

Audience and ad settings can differ by app. Some games are designed for children and families and use child-directed / Families-appropriate advertising settings. Other games are intended for a general audience. Each app’s Google Play listing and in-app ad configuration describe that app’s audience.

Request Deletion of Your Data

You can remove data as follows:

  1. Game data on the device: open your device Settings → Apps → select the game → Storage → Clear storage / Clear data (wording varies by device), or uninstall the app. This removes locally saved progress and settings.
  2. Advertising data processed by Google: Google may process data when ads are shown. Use Google’s privacy policy and Google’s ad controls for options available through Google.
  3. Contact me: email grapegames3141@gmail.com with the subject “Grapegames data deletion request,” name the app, and I will respond with these steps or answer questions.

Because I do not operate user accounts or store your game progress on my servers, there is no separate Grapegames account profile to delete.

Children’s Privacy

Where an app is offered for children or families, it is configured to follow applicable Google Play Families requirements and does not knowingly collect children’s personal information on Grapegames servers. Local play data remains on the device as described above. Child-directed apps use Google’s child-directed / Families-appropriate ad settings and do not use behavioral advertising targeted at children in those apps.

General-audience apps are not directed at children. Parents who believe a child has provided personal information to Google through ads or Play services should use Google’s tools linked above or contact me at the email listed here.

Security

Data transmitted to Google for ads or Play services is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Local game data on your device is protected by your device’s operating system.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

I may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the apps after an update means you accept the revised policy.
